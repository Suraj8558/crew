Welcome to Parichy Elevate Your Digital Identity

At Parichy, we make it easy for individuals and businesses to establish a powerful online presence. From dynamic digital business cards to tailored websites, and multifunctional QR codes, Parichy offers a suite of tools to help you make an impact and engage effortlessly. Whether you’re a freelancer, a business owner, or a professional, Parichy’s innovative services are designed to give you total control over your digital identity.

Our Features

Dynamic URL & vCard+

Update your digital business card (vCard+) in real-time with a customizable, dynamic URL. Share your contact info, showcase your portfolio, and leave a lasting impression—wherever you go.

Bio Links & Business Review

Optimize your online presence with Bio Links that connect all your social platforms and sites in one easy-to-share link. Collect valuable reviews that build your reputation with our Business Review feature.

Website Builder & Business Profile

Create a professional, fully functional website without any coding. Showcase your story, products, and services with a customizable Business Profile, giving your brand an impressive digital footprint

Lead Forms & Catalogues:

Capture leads directly from your profile or digital business card with easy-to-integrate forms. Showcase your products and services in a sleek Product Catalogue for quick and effective customer browsing.

Restaurant Menus & Google Review Collector

Go digital with interactive, QR-code-enabled Restaurant Menus. Plus, easily gather and manage Google reviews to enhance your business reputation.

Resume Builder & Event Pages

Perfect for professionals, our Resume Builder gives you a digital resume you can share with a click. Create dedicated pages for events and keep your audience informed and engaged.

File Upload & Customizable QR Codes

Share documents, brochures, and more with a simple File Upload option. Generate and customize QR codes for everything from static text and URLs to SMS, email, and location sharing.
